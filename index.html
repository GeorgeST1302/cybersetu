How a complaint moves

Five stages, and you can see all of them.

Every complaint follows the same path. Once you file, your own case shows you which stage it has reached and what is expected next.

STAGE 01

Complaint received

Your report is logged and a reference number is issued to you straight away.

Immediate
STAGE 02

Bank hold requested

For financial fraud, a hold request goes to the beneficiary bank to stop the money moving on.

Within the hour
STAGE 03

Assigned to an officer

The complaint is routed to the cyber cell responsible for your district.

1–3 days
STAGE 04

Under investigation

The officer gathers records, contacts banks or platforms, and follows the leads.

Varies by case
STAGE 05

Outcome recorded

You are told the result, and what happens to any amount that was held.
