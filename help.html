Your information

What we ask for, and why.

We only ask for what moves your case

Every field on the complaint form exists because an officer or a bank needs it. Anything optional is marked optional, and you can submit without it.

Your draft stays on your device

A part-finished complaint is saved in your own browser so you do not lose it. It is not sent anywhere until you press submit.

Evidence is fingerprinted, not altered

Each file you attach gets a SHA-256 fingerprint calculated in your browser. It travels with the complaint so it can be shown the file was not changed afterwards.

Anonymous reporting stays anonymous

For complaints involving a woman or child, you can file without giving your name or number. Your reference number is then the only link to the complaint.
