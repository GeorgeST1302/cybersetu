Check it before you pay.

Paste a phone number, UPI ID, email address or link. We match it against identifiers other people have reported, so you can pause before the money leaves.

How to read this

This checks what has been reported, not what is provably criminal. A high score means many people have flagged it and you should not proceed. A clean result means nobody has reported it yet — which is not the same as being safe.

Warn the next person

Report a number, ID or link you were targeted with.

You do not need to have lost money. Flagging an identifier helps the next person get a warning before they pay.
